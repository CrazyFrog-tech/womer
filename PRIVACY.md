# Privacy Policy for Womer

**Effective Date:** 2026-03-04

Thank you for using Womer, a workout timer application. Your privacy is important to us. This privacy policy explains how your information is handled within the app.

## Information We Do Not Collect

Womer is designed to be a simple, standalone utility that runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information.

- **No Personal Data:** We do not ask for, access, or collect any personal information such as your name, email address, location, or contacts.
- **No Usage Data:** We do not collect analytics or data about how you use the app.
- **Local Storage Only:** All data created by the app, such as your saved timer configurations (work time, rest time, and sets), is stored exclusively on your local device. This data is not accessible to us and is not sent to any server.

## Permissions

Womer may request the following permissions on Android to function correctly:

- **Post Notifications:** This permission is required to display timer alerts and updates while the app is running in the background, ensuring you don't miss an interval. We do not use this permission for marketing or any other purpose.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
